01 / ABOUT

A strategic mind with tactical purpose.

I believe great defending isn't only about making spectacular tackles. It is about reading the game, commanding the pitch, and building a foundation your team can rely on.

Rúben Dias is a Portuguese centre-back known for his defensive leadership, positioning, and right-footed play.

Born in Amadora on 14 May 1997, I developed my leadership and game intelligence at SL Benfica before joining Manchester City in 2020. Grounded in composure, tactical discipline, and relentless determination, my goal is to lead from the back and deliver consistent excellence.
